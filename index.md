## 🧠 How It Works (Without Technical Jargon)

You might be wondering what all those big words in the description mean. Don't worry—it's actually quite simple when broken down:ta

- **C++ Arena –** This is the "engine room" of the program. It's where the computer simulates thousands of fast practice games against itself. Think of it like a chess computer that plays millions of matches to learn the best openings.



- **GBDT Imitation –** After playing those practice games, the computer watches which moves led to victories and copies those strategies. It's like learning by copying a champion player's technique, but with the ability to process millions of examples instantly.ta


- **Transformer Distillation –** This clever method takes all that knowledge from the imitation phase and compresses it into a "cheat sheet" that the program can use in real-time without slowing down. Instead of thinking through every possibility, it quickly consults its distilled knowledge to suggest a good move.ta


- **PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization) –** The final layer is a "fine-tuner." It continuously tweaks the strategies based on what works best against different opponents. It's like having a coach who adjusts your game plan after every match depending on your opponent's style.ta

Together, these four layers work seamlessly in the background to give you the most accurate, helpful advice possible. The interface never shows you any of the complex math—all you see are clear recommendations and explanations in simple English.ta
